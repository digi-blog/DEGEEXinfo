01 / THE AIRFRAME

One aircraft.
Two missions.

The F/A-18C is a single-seat, twin-engine multirole fighter built to operate from aircraft carriers.

Its recognizable planform combines swept wings, leading-edge extensions, twin vertical stabilizers, and folding outer wing panels. These features support maneuverability and practical storage aboard ship.

03 / WEAPONS & STORES

A flexible loadout.

The legacy F/A-18C could be cleared for several families of weapons and support stores. This is a reference to publicly documented store types, not a mission configuration: compatibility changed with aircraft block, software, operator, and service date.

01 / INTERNAL GUN

M61A1 VULCAN

A 20 mm, six-barrel rotary cannon installed internally in the nose. Unlike the interchangeable external stores below, it is part of the aircraft’s fixed armament.

02 / AIR-TO-AIR

AIM-7 SPARROW

A medium-range, semi-active radar-homing missile family carried by compatible Hornet variants. Sparrow versions and aircraft integration changed over time, so the exact capability depended on the aircraft’s upgrade and service period.

03 / AIR-TO-AIR

AIM-120 AMRAAM

An active-radar-guided missile family with inertial navigation and data-link updates during midcourse flight. It provided a newer air-to-air option on compatible Hornets; operational capability depended on avionics and software upgrades.

04 / AIR-TO-AIR

AIM-9 SIDEWINDER

A short-range, infrared-guided missile family. F/A-18C service included earlier AIM-9 variants and later AIM-9X integrations; seeker and aircraft capabilities varied by missile version and upgrade.

05 / AIR-TO-SURFACE

AGM-65 MAVERICK

The AGM-65F variant documented on the Hornet used an imaging-infrared seeker. Maverick is an air-to-surface missile family; seeker type and aircraft compatibility varied across variants and upgrades.

06 / AIR-TO-SURFACE

AGM-84 HARPOON & SLAM

Harpoon is the anti-ship member of the AGM-84 family; AGM-84E SLAM is a related land-attack variant. SLAM-ER is a distinct, improved derivative and is covered separately below.

07 / AIR-TO-SURFACE

AGM-88 HARM

The High-speed Anti-Radiation Missile is an air-to-surface weapon designed to home on radar emissions. HARM version support and aircraft compatibility varied with the Hornet’s block, software, and service period.

08 / STAND-OFF STORES

AGM-154 JSOW

The Joint Standoff Weapon is an unpowered glide weapon with GPS-aided/inertial navigation. The AGM-154 family includes different variants; Hornet compatibility depended on aircraft block and software upgrade.

09 / STAND-OFF STORES

AGM-84H SLAM-ER

An improved AGM-84 land-attack variant designed for precision strike. Guidance and compatibility depended on the specific missile configuration and the aircraft’s approved avionics and software fit.

10 / BOMBS

MK 80, PAVEWAY & JDAM

Documented bomb families include unguided Mk 80-series bombs, laser-guided Paveway variants, and GPS/INS-guided JDAM variants on compatible aircraft. The photograph is a historical example, not a recommended configuration.

11 / SUPPORT STORES

EXTERNAL FUEL TANKS

External tanks carry additional fuel to extend the aircraft’s endurance. They are support stores rather than weapons; tank use and approved combinations varied with aircraft configuration and operator requirements.

12 / MISSION PODS

TARGETING PODS

Electro-optical/infrared targeting pods provide imagery and may include laser designation. ATFLIR is one U.S. Navy example; the photo shows pod maintenance, and the pod types cleared for a Hornet varied by block, upgrade, and operator.

PUBLIC-HISTORY OVERVIEW · Not every listed store was cleared for every F/A-18C, and not all stores could be carried together. Photo links open their source pages on Wikimedia Commons; captions identify the photographer or source and license.
